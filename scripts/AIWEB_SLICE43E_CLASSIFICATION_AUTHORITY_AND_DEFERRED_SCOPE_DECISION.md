# AI.Web Slice 43E Classification Authority and Deferred Scope Decision

## Ruling

Slice 43E is authorized only to classify exact admitted drift kinds and record
deterministic materiality findings from validated Slice 43D comparison records.

## Admitted input

The only admitted input is a valid Slice 43D findings-created result whose
canonical identities, package identities, finding identities and exact
predecessor custody validate under the committed Slice 43D law.

Raw text, free-form interpretation, recomputed identities, unsupported
versions and inconsistent ancestry are rejected from classification.

## Admitted output

The output is an immutable findings package containing one classification
record per Slice 43D dimension finding. A record may contain zero, one or
multiple admitted drift kinds. Preserved, unsupported, conflicted and
indeterminate comparison states remain explicit.

## Materiality

Materiality is a finding, not a disposition. Materiality does not itself grant
approval, rejection, containment, repair, delivery or action authority.

Controlled formatting-only additions may be classified non-material. Other
unsupported surface additions remain indeterminate unless another admitted
material drift kind establishes materiality.
