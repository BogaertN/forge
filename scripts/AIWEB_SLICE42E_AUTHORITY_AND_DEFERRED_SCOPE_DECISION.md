# Slice 42E Authority and Deferred-Scope Decision

## Admitted authority

Slice 42E admits only a separate, explicit, active, versioned and receipt-bound `ExpressionPlanConstructionAuthorityRecord` tied to the exact Slice 42D input, result and obligation package.

The authority may permit:

- an affirmative plan only when the exact source state is `eligible_for_expression_planning`;
- a nonaffirmative containment plan for blocked, refusal-preserving or unresolved-preserving states.

A containment plan does not upgrade source eligibility and may not assert the blocked selected meaning as an authorized claim.

## Deferred authority

The following remain unauthorized:

- human-readable wording;
- surface realization;
- expression-candidate creation;
- governed-outward-meaning MSM integration;
- MSM-v1 mutation;
- Echo validation;
- delivery;
- truth, evidence, permission or execution authority;
- routes, APIs, network, filesystem, memory, resources, tools or actions;
- LLM, embedding, vector, RAG, similarity, neural parser or hidden-classifier authority;
- GP-014 supersession.

## Binding ordering rule

Higher-order semantic and authority restrictions govern every lower-order plan section. No modifier, qualification, caveat, refusal or wording choice may erase, weaken or override those restrictions.
