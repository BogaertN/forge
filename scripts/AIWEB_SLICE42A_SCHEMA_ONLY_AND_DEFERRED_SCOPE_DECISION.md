# AI.Web Slice 42A Schema-Only and Deferred-Scope Decision

## Accepted scope

Slice 42A may add immutable, frozen, slotted schema contracts that preserve the
exact boundary between:

1. accepted selected-meaning custody;
2. outward-expression authority requirements;
3. preservation obligations;
4. expression-eligibility status;
5. governed outward meaning;
6. expression planning;
7. realized expression;
8. expression trace and receipt custody.

It may also bind the exact accepted Slice 41 parent identity and record the
permanent non-authority laws governing later Slice 42 increments.

## Required preservation custody

The schema must separately carry references for:

- active scope;
- certainty level;
- evidence status;
- inherited limitations;
- required caveats;
- refusal-relevant boundaries;
- unresolved conditions;
- memory authority;
- external-resource status;
- delivery authority.

It must also preserve selected-candidate identity, selected-candidate lineage,
semantic content, alternatives, non-selection outcomes, ambiguity ancestry,
clarification ancestry, blocked consequences, refusal relevance,
authority-sensitive distinctions, and semantic preservation classes.

## Deferred scope

The following remain deferred:

- 42B deterministic validation, identity, canonical serialization, versioning,
  duplicate and collision rejection, lifecycle, and cross-record consistency;
- 42C exact authority admission and expression-eligibility evaluation;
- 42D preservation-obligation projection;
- 42E governed outward-meaning and expression-plan construction;
- 42F deterministic surface realization, realization trace, and receipt;
- 42G additive MSM-v1 outward-meaning and expression-link custody;
- 42H disabled bootstrap integration and Slice 42 closeout;
- Slice 43 deterministic RMC Echo validation;
- any later delivery or action authority.

## Permanent boundaries

- Selected meaning is not expression authority.
- Selected meaning alone is not expression eligibility.
- Selected meaning may not be rewritten.
- Alternatives and non-selection outcomes may not be deleted.
- Unresolved state and ambiguity may not be silently collapsed.
- Uncertainty and evidence status may not be upgraded.
- Inherited limitations and caveats may not disappear.
- Refusal may not be softened into permission.
- Memory references do not grant memory authority.
- External-resource references do not grant resource admission.
- Delivery references do not grant delivery authority.
- Governed outward meaning is not an expression plan.
- An expression plan is not realized text.
- A realized expression is not Echo validation.
- Echo validation belongs to Slice 43.
- Echo validation is not delivery.
- No LLM, embedding, vector, RAG, similarity, neural parser, hidden classifier,
  route, API, network, filesystem, memory write, tool, action, or delivery.
- MSM-v1 is not rewritten.
- GP-014 is not superseded.
