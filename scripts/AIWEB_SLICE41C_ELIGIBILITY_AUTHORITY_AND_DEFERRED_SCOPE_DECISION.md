# Slice 41C Eligibility Authority and Deferred Scope Decision

## Decision

Slice 41C is implemented as an additive versioned companion runtime under `selected_meaning_runtime/eligibility_evaluation`. It does not alter the accepted MSM-v1, Slice 39G, Slice 40G, Slice 40H, Slice 41A or Slice 41B schemas.

The evaluator must cross-validate all exact predecessor identities and references before producing an eligibility result. No summary, string resemblance, candidate count, convenience fallback or implicit authority may substitute for those exact records.

## Explicit positive support

`eligible_for_selected_meaning_construction` requires the exact candidate-specific Slice 40G disposition `candidate_supported_for_later_selection_review`, an approved authority profile, no material unresolved or adverse custody, and explicit positive-support references bound to the candidate.

## Adverse precedence

The deterministic precedence is:

1. conflicted;
2. unsupported;
3. clarification-dependent;
4. materially unresolved;
5. held pending authority;
6. explicitly not eligible;
7. indeterminate;
8. eligible only when every positive condition is satisfied.

## Deferred authority

Slice 41C does not construct selected meaning, choose between candidate meanings, mutate MSM-v1, activate bootstrap integration, validate truth or evidence, convert meaning into permission, invoke a route or tool, perform an action, access memory, render output or deliver anything.
