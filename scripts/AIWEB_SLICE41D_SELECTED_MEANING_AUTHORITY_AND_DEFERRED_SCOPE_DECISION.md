# Slice 41D Selected-Meaning Authority and Deferred-Scope Decision

## Authorized operation

Slice 41D may construct exactly one governed selected-meaning package only when the supplied Slice 41C result is the exact validated outcome `eligible_for_selected_meaning_construction` for the exact supplied Slice 41C evaluation input.

## Required preservation

The constructor must preserve:

- exact selected candidate identity and lineage;
- exact candidate semantic content without enrichment or deletion;
- exact candidate companion custody;
- candidate authority-sensitive implications;
- inherited limitations and blocked-consequence markers;
- every non-selected candidate by exact reference;
- unresolved alternatives separately;
- ambiguity and clarification ancestry;
- refusal relevance and blocked progression;
- deterministic trace and receipt identities.

## Permanent distinctions

- selected candidate is not the only candidate that ever existed;
- selected meaning is not truth;
- selected meaning is not evidence;
- selected meaning is not permission;
- selected meaning is not execution;
- selected meaning is not an outward answer;
- selected meaning is not delivery;
- selected meaning is not MSM-v1 integration.

## Explicitly prohibited

Candidate ranking, confidence or probability scoring, similarity, nearest-known substitution, hidden classification, LLM use, automatic only/first/safest candidate selection, alternative deletion, semantic enrichment, semantic deletion, MSM-v1 mutation, outward meaning, truth/evidence/permission/execution authority, routes, tools, actions, memory, rendering, delivery, external resources, and bootstrap activation are prohibited.

## Deferred

Slice 41E owns immutable MSM-v1 successor integration and custody. Later slices own outward meaning, expression, validation, and delivery.
