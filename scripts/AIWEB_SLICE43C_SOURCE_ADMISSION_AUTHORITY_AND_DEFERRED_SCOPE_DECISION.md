# AI.Web Slice 43C Source-Admission Authority and Deferred-Scope Decision

## Decision

Slice 43C may admit one exact, closed, accepted Slice 42 ancestry package into
RMC Echo input custody. Admission is structural and identity-bound. It does not
compare meaning, classify drift, decide an Echo disposition, or authorize
output delivery.

The only admissible source is the exact accepted Slice 42H closeout result and
its exact linked Slice 41E and Slice 42C through 42G records. The admitted chain
must contain the exact selected governed meaning, governed outward meaning,
realized unvalidated expression candidate, MSM-v1 expression link, source and
successor manifests, deterministic traces, receipts, custody records, versions,
and lineage.

## Mandatory rejection law

Slice 43C rejects or holds:

- arbitrary raw text, even when supplied beside an accepted source;
- an orphan expression or an expression link that does not bind the exact
  outward meaning and candidate;
- recomputed, substituted, or fabricated record identities;
- unsupported schema or profile versions;
- missing selected-meaning, outward-meaning, candidate, link, trace, receipt,
  manifest, authority, or custody references;
- an already delivered, delivery-authorized, contained, or validation-linked
  candidate;
- a candidate without exact accepted realization authority;
- conflicting lineage or cross-record ancestry;
- any malformed request, duplicate source, or identity collision.

No nearest-known substitution, silent repair, inference from raw text, or
identity reconstruction is permitted.

## Admission is not validation success

The following implications are permanently rejected:

`source admitted -> meaning preserved`

`source admitted -> Echo PASSED`

`source admitted -> delivery authorized`

`structural identity valid -> semantic equivalence`

Slice 43C creates only a deterministic admission package for later Slice 43D
comparison. The existing Slice 43A input boundary remains immutable and does
not retroactively gain authority. A Slice 43C companion records that the exact
source has been admitted for later comparison.

## Permanent authority-zero boundary

Slice 43C does not:

- inspect or interpret arbitrary raw text;
- compare authorized meaning with proposed expression;
- create validation findings or drift findings;
- classify drift or materiality;
- decide `PASSED`, `REJECTED`, or `CONTAINED`;
- issue rejection or containment records;
- repair, rewrite, paraphrase, summarize, or regenerate expression text;
- modify, migrate, or integrate MSM-v1;
- create an MSM-v1 validation or delivery link;
- enable bootstrap integration;
- authorize or perform delivery;
- determine truth, validate evidence, grant permission, or authorize execution;
- create routes or APIs, access network or filesystem, write memory, invoke a
  tool, perform an action, or load external resources;
- use EchoForge, an LLM, embedding, vector, RAG, similarity engine, neural
  parser, hidden classifier, confidence score, probability, or nearest-known
  substitution;
- supersede GP-014.
