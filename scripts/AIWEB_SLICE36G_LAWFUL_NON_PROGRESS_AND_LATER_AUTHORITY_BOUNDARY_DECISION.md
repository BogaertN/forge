# AI.Web Slice 36G — Lawful Non-Progress and Later Authority Boundary Decision

## Decision

Slice 36G is adopted as a deterministic structural-analysis and lawful non-progress layer only.

It may combine exact accepted Slice 36A through Slice 36F records into zero, one or multiple bounded structural-analysis candidates. It may preserve explicit malformed, unsupported, ambiguous, incomplete, drift-contained, recursion-suspended and prohibited-context states.

It may not cross the boundary into meaning selection, gate disposition or outward behavior.

## Binding distinctions

The following distinctions are permanent:

- structural candidate is not CandidateMeaning;
- structural plurality is not semantic ambiguity disposition;
- unresolved structure is not a request for clarification;
- unsupported structure is not semantic rejection;
- malformed structure is not refusal-relevant interpretation;
- source coverage is not truth coverage;
- reconstruction proof is not evidence validity;
- reference candidate is not reference resolution;
- operator graph is not concept graph;
- action-like source signal is not predicate identity;
- constrained imperative surface is not permission;
- recognized capability wording is not authorized capability;
- non-progress is not failure and must not be guessed away.

## Slice 39 reservation

Slice 39 exclusively owns construction of CandidateMeaning authority from later-approved concept, sense, predicate and participant-role records. Slice 36G must not create a `CandidateMeaning`, a substitute meaning record or any field that claims equivalent authority.

## Slice 40 reservation

Slice 40 exclusively owns gate evaluation, ambiguity disposition, clarification-required status, rejection, unsupported-language disposition, blocked progression and refusal-relevant interpretation. Slice 36G records structural conditions only and must not ask the user a clarification question or choose a gate outcome.

## Operational prohibition

Slice 36G grants no permission, capability, route, tool, action, memory, rendering, delivery or release authority. A complete structural candidate still has no world-state consequence.

## Failure discipline

Invalid ancestry, invalid limits, malformed records or internal contract violations fail closed. The runtime returns typed failure metadata without source mutation, candidate selection, semantic inference or hidden fallback.
