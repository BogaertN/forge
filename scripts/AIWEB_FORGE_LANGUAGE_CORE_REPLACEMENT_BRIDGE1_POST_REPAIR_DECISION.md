# Forge Language-Core Replacement Bridge 1 Decision

## Accepted architecture

Forge remains the workshop and authority boundary. The language core replaces
the historical model interpretation role incrementally. RMC remains the
recursive manifest/memory spine and is not reduced to a chat transcript.

## Bridge 1 decision

Bridge 1 activates only exact deterministic request custody and bounded route
selection for a small existing command family. It does not claim that the full
selected-meaning runtime or full μ_t compiler is connected.

## Preserved boundaries

- The bridge cannot call Ollama or any model.
- The bridge cannot execute shell.
- The bridge cannot invoke arbitrary tools.
- The bridge cannot run a simulation.
- The bridge cannot grant permission.
- The bridge cannot write source or RMC memory.
- Existing Forge command functions and approval gates remain authoritative.
- Unsupported requests remain on the historical fallback until a later bridge
  is separately applied and accepted.
