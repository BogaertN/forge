# Slice 37B Lifecycle-Authority and Deferred-Scope Decision

## Accepted authority

Slice 37B may determine whether an explicitly supplied immutable governance
record set conforms to the accepted Slice 37A schema and Document 4 lifecycle
law. It may recompute IDs, validate fields, inspect exact in-memory references,
evaluate a proposed transition and reject a non-conforming batch.

## Not accepted

Slice 37B does not:

- admit any named concept, sense, lexical reference, mapping, class or relation;
- maintain a persistent registry or lifecycle ledger;
- discover or infer a transition;
- decide that an external source is authoritative;
- incorporate Document 8 resource decisions;
- interpret a source occurrence;
- select a concept, sense or meaning;
- consume Slice 36 structural candidates;
- create Predicate or CandidateMeaning authority;
- expose an API, capability route, tool, memory operation or action;
- render or deliver output;
- declare runtime, release or production status.

## Historical preservation

The Slice 8 concept-boundary scaffold remains unchanged, scaffold-only and
unsuperseded. Slice 37A remains unchanged and hash-protected. Slice 37B extends
37A through an explicit subpackage and does not alter the parent package export
surface.

## Deferred sequence

Registry population remains Slice 37C. Controlled sense and exact term mapping
remain Slice 37D. Semantic classes and relation-type rules remain Slice 37E.
Structural-to-concept candidate proposal remains Slice 37F. Disabled integration
and Slice 37 closeout remain Slice 37G.
