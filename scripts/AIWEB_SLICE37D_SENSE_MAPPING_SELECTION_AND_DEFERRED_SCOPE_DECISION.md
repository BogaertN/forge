# Slice 37D Sense/Mapping Selection and Deferred-Scope Decision

## Selection basis

The first Slice 37D set is intentionally small and architecture-facing. Every admitted lexical reference is grounded in the exact Slice 37C concept labels/keys or in a bounded Document 4 proof condition. Frequency, dictionary familiarity, source repetition, historical fixtures, similarity, and implementation convenience were disqualified as admission grounds.

## Why these five senses

The set proves the distinctions needed before later meaning construction:

- semantic resource use versus expression mention;
- source-bound occurrence versus reusable lexical reference;
- concept admission versus implementation/runtime authorization;
- unknown concept support versus guessed substitute meaning.

## Why these eleven lexical references

The eight Slice 37C labels/keys prove exact outward and internal-namespace mappings. The exact domain term `concept` proves one-to-many ambiguity. The exact term `mapping` proves that a known lexical reference may remain unmapped. The exact term `sense` proves that a mapping review may end unsupported without fabricating a concept candidate.

## Why no broader vocabulary

No ordinary-language vocabulary, synonym set, morphological family, misspelling table, acronym expansion, dictionary entry, external lexical record, Slice 8 fixture, BUILD-009 label, or project-history term is admitted. Such additions require later exact authority and a separately approved patch.

## Deferred scope

- Semantic classes and semantic relations remain deferred to Slice 37E.
- Structural-result integration remains deferred to Slice 37F.
- CandidateMeaning construction remains deferred to Slice 39.
- Ambiguity disposition, clarification, rejection, and blocked progression remain deferred to Slice 40.
- Outward realization, validation, and delivery remain separately governed.
- External-resource acquisition and promotion remain separately governed.

## Predecessor preservation

Slice 37C concept records are not rewritten. Sense-to-concept custody is represented by each controlled sense identity's exact `concept_id` and by the Slice 37D registry manifest. This preserves the accepted Slice 37C tree while adding the separately governed Slice 37D semantic-resource layer.
