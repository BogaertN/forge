# Slice 38E Frame Admission and Deferred-Scope Decision

## Admitted frame identities

- `inspect_read_only`
- `report_attributed_content`
- `request_non_authorizing`
- `verify_bounded_review`
- `simulate_non_live`

These are architecture-level frame identities only. They are anchored to the exact action roots and predicate identities admitted by Slice 38C and constrained by the exact participant-role identities admitted by Slice 38D.

## Role-compatibility ruling

Every admitted role receives one frame-specific role constraint and one frame-specific concept-compatibility rule. The compatibility rule may reference admitted Slice 37E semantic classes as architecture review filters, but class membership is never sufficient. Exact concept allowlists remain unpopulated. No concept candidate, source span, grammatical position, semantic relation, or surface term becomes a role assignment.

## Structural-state ruling

Slice 38E defines policy records for:

- structurally complete
- structurally incomplete
- ambiguous
- conflicted
- unsupported
- unknown

These are structural states, not Document 6 gate outcomes. Structurally complete is not permission.

## Deferred frame families

Approval, installation, delivery, memory mutation, rollback execution, external-resource admission, economic consequence, and identity-verification frames remain deferred. Capability-family references and full effect-boundary records remain Slice 38F work.
