## Canonical compatibility ruling

The currently admitted Slice 37 concepts and senses are meta-semantic identities. None is definitionally identical to `inspect`, `report`, `request`, `verify`, or `simulate`. The canonical compatibility snapshot is therefore intentionally closed and empty. Current canonical Slice 37 candidate-bearing inputs become explicit unsupported state rather than being silently mapped. Positive candidate behavior is testable only through separately supplied exact immutable compatibility snapshots; those snapshots remain candidate-only and carry no selection authority.

## Non-authority

Those consequences remain reserved to later governed layers.
