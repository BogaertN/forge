# AI.Web Slice 39E Authority and Deferred-Scope Decision

## Accepted authority

Slice 39E owns deterministic custody of zero, one, or multiple exact Slice 39D
candidate semantic-content records. It may order records canonically, detect
exact duplicate result identities, preserve every duplicate occurrence, retain
exact pairwise difference dimensions, preserve shared ancestry, and keep
candidate-specific limitations, missing roles, conflicts, effect boundaries,
and capability references attached to their source candidate.

For this slice, **material alternative** means only that two valid candidate
records differ in at least one exact governed content or provenance dimension.
It is not a verbal-cognition judgment and does not mean the alternatives are
ambiguous.

## Permanent boundary

Deterministic position is not rank. Exact duplicate detection is not semantic
similarity. One candidate is not automatically selected. Multiple candidates do
not automatically create `AmbiguousMeaningState`. Shared ancestry is not lineage
merger. No score, confidence, preference, winner, tie-break, automatic ambiguity
resolution, gate result, selected meaning, truth, evidence, permission, route,
action, memory, rendering, or delivery authority is created.

39G manifest integration result, and is not a Slice 39H acceptance record.
