# AI.Web Slice 39E Authority and Deferred-Scope Decision

## Accepted authority

Slice 39E owns deterministic custody of zero, one, or multiple exact Slice 39D candidate semantic-content records. It may order candidate records canonically, detect exact duplicate result identities, preserve every duplicate occurrence explicitly, retain exact pairwise difference dimensions, preserve shared ancestry, and keep candidate-specific limitations, missing roles, conflicts, effect boundaries, and capability references attached to the candidate that carried them.

For this slice, **material alternative** has one narrow meaning: two valid candidate records differ in at least one exact governed content or provenance dimension. It is not a verbal-cognition judgment and does not mean the alternatives are ambiguous.

## Permanent boundary

Deterministic position is not rank. Exact duplicate detection is not semantic similarity. One candidate is not automatically selected. Multiple candidates do not automatically create `AmbiguousMeaningState`. Shared ancestry is not lineage merger. No score, confidence, preference, best candidate, nearest candidate, winner, tie-break, automatic ambiguity resolution, gate result, selected meaning, truth, evidence, permission, route, action, memory, rendering, or delivery authority is created.

Slice 40 retains expectancy, congruity, connectedness, recoverable-purpose evaluation, material-ambiguity determination, clarification, holding, rejection, blocked progression, and selection. Later governed layers retain selected meaning and every consequence beyond interpretation custody.
