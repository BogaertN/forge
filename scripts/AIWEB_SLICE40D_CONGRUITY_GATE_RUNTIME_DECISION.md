# Slice 40D Congruity Gate Runtime Decision

## Decision

Install a deterministic, read-only congruity evaluator over exact compatibility
assertions admitted by predecessor concept, sense, semantic-class, predicate,
frame, role, speech-act, effect-boundary, domain-marker, and capability-reference
authority.

## Permitted outcomes

The family evaluator may return compatible, incompatible, ambiguous,
unsupported, conflicted, or indeterminate. These are family-level evaluation
states only. They are not candidate rejection, clarification, refusal,
blocked progression, overall gate disposition, or selected meaning.

## Required controls

- one exact observation for every explicit compatibility assertion;
- deterministic canonical serialization and SHA-256 identities;
- sealed Slice 40B governance and congruity-family custody;
- preservation of candidate, predicate, frame, trace, provenance, and limitation
  references;
- immutable inputs and results;
- no raw-text interpretation;
- no similarity, nearest-known substitution, hidden model judgment, or silent
  repair;
- no frame rewrite or role reassignment;
- no capability-driven selection;
- no truth, evidence, permission, execution, memory, routing, rendering, or
  delivery authority.

## Explicit non-decision

Slice 40D does not decide how the four gate families compose. It does not name
or create the positive Slice 40G selection-review disposition. It does not
select meaning. Those authorities remain deferred.
