# Slice 42D Authority and Deferred-Scope Decision

## Accepted authority

Slice 42D is authorized only to project exact preservation obligations from an exact validated Slice 42C state when a separate exact projection-authority record is present and valid.

Projection authority is not inferred from:

- selected meaning;
- Slice 42C eligibility;
- structural validity;
- expression-planning progression eligibility;
- an outward-expression authority record;
- source usefulness;
- runtime capability existence.

## Binding preservation ruling

The obligation package must retain, without semantic upgrade or deletion, selected meaning, active scope, certainty, evidence status, inherited limitations, caveats, refusal boundaries, unresolved conditions, ambiguity, unsupported states, memory status, external-resource status and delivery status.

## Deferred authority

The following remain outside Slice 42D:

- governed outward-meaning construction;
- expression-plan construction;
- expression ordering or lexical choice;
- surface realization or human-readable output;
- Echo validation;
- delivery;
- memory operations;
- external-resource acquisition or loading;
- routes, APIs, tools and actions;
- truth, evidence, permission or execution authority;
- model, embedding, vector, RAG, similarity or hidden-classifier authority;
- GP-014 supersession.

## Next lawful slice

Slice 42E may consume an accepted Slice 42D obligation package to construct a deterministic controlled expression plan. Slice 42E may not omit or weaken any accepted Slice 42D obligation.
