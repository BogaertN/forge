## Purpose

Slice 42D creates one exact immutable expression-obligation package from an exact validated Slice 42C authorized-meaning and expression-eligibility state. Projection is separately authorized by an explicit, active, versioned, scoped, receipt-bound preservation-projection authority record.

The package separately preserves:

1. active scope;
2. certainty level;
3. evidence status;
4. inherited limitations;
5. required caveats;
6. refusal-relevant boundaries;
7. unresolved conditions;
8. memory authority status;
9. external-resource status;
10. delivery authority status.

It also preserves selected-meaning references, ambiguity references, unsupported-state references, privacy/identity boundaries, preservation classes, predecessor receipts, trace, provenance and version custody.

## Exact authority boundary

Slice 42C eligibility does not imply Slice 42D projection authority. Slice 42D requires a separate `PreservationObligationProjectionAuthorityRecord` whose identities and references match the exact Slice 42C input/result chain.

A structurally valid record does not grant authority. A recomputed identifier does not cure altered scope, status, lineage, receipt or obligation content.
