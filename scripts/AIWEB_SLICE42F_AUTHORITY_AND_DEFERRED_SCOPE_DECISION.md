# AI.Web Slice 42F — Authority and Deferred-Scope Decision

## Admitted authority

Slice 42F admits only a separate, explicit, versioned and receipt-bound
surface-realization authority.

That authority may authorize:

- deterministic realization of an exact accepted Slice 42E plan;
- use of exact admitted realization rules;
- use of exact controlled internal resources;
- creation of one unvalidated expression candidate;
- creation of one deterministic realization trace;
- creation of one deterministic realization receipt.

## Refused authority

Slice 42F does not infer realization authority from:

- selected meaning;
- outward-expression eligibility;
- preservation-obligation projection;
- expression-plan construction;
- structural validity;
- the existence of a template or resource.

## Deferred scope

The following remain deferred:

- governed outward-meaning MSM integration;
- Echo validation or approval;
- delivery authorization or delivery;
- memory read or write;
- external-resource acquisition or loading;
- route, API, tool or action authority;
- truth, evidence, permission or execution authority;
- bootstrap activation;
- GP-014 supersession.

The realized text remains an unvalidated expression candidate.
